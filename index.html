About Me

Data-Driven. Detail-Obsessed. Deadline-Ready.

I'm a Data Executive at Automate Accelerator, where I specialise in building B2B prospect lists for Australian client campaigns. My work spans the full data pipeline — from sourcing companies through Seamless, Google Maps Scraper and business directories, to verifying each business against specific target audience criteria, enriching records with addresses, postcodes, LinkedIn profiles and contact details, and delivering clean datasets ready for CRM import.

Prior to this role, I completed a Data Analytics internship at Tata1k Cares, where I mapped data workflows, converted unstructured information into structured datasets and prepared analytical reports to support business decisions.

With a postgraduate engineering background (M.Tech, Heritage Institute of Technology) and professional certifications in Data Analytics and Python, I bring analytical precision and technical depth to every project. I'm proficient in Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI, Pandas and NumPy, and I work daily with lead-generation tools including Seamless, ContactOut, SignalHire and RocketReach.
