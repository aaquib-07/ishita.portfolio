About Me

Building B2B Databases
That Drive Results

I'm a Data Executive at Automate Accelerator, where I build verified B2B prospect lists for Australian client projects. My work sits at the intersection of meticulous research and data engineering — I source companies through business directories, evaluate them against specific target audience criteria, enrich records with addresses, LinkedIn profiles and contact details, and deliver clean, CRM-ready datasets.

With a background in data analytics from my internship at Tata1k Cares and postgraduate training in engineering, I bring analytical rigour to every dataset I build. I'm proficient in Python, SQL, Excel and Power BI, and I regularly work with lead-generation platforms like Seamless, ContactOut, SignalHire and RocketReach.

I'm passionate about data quality — deduplication, validation and enrichment aren't just steps in my workflow, they're what set my output apart.
